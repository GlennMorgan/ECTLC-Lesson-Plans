ECTLC Personal Safety Plan

Personal safety is part of the Protect stage of the Rhombus Model continuum of care: Protect → Prepare → Propel. Safety creates the structure needed for stability, growth, employment readiness, and long-term recovery.

Why Safety Matters

  • Safety protects recovery before motivation becomes strong
  • Safety helps stabilize emotions and decisions
  • Safety strengthens housing readiness
  • Safety supports movement toward responsibility and independence

Four Practical Areas Where Recovery Safety Must Be Built

  • Sleep schedule
  • Substance-free environment
  • Health routines
  • Following program structure

Rhombus Model Reminder

Safety is how recovery is protected first. When safety becomes consistent, people can prepare for responsibility. When responsibility becomes consistent, people can move forward into employment, housing stability, and long-term recovery.
