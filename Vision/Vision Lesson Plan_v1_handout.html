Vision to Goals

In-Class Reflection and Group Exercise

Purpose:
Vision gives direction. Goals create movement. This exercise turns one vision insight into small, doable actions.

Step 1: Identify Your Vision Nugget

Write your ONE vision nugget (a sentence or phrase that matters to you):

Step 2: Small Group Goal Development (Groups of 4)

Group Instructions:
  • One person shares their vision nugget.
  • The group brainstorms possible actions that support the vision.
  • Focus on behaviors the person can control.
  • No advice, no pressure, no “you should.”
Helpful prompts:
  • What is one small step that points in this direction?
  • What habit or practice would support this vision?
  • What could this look like on a normal day?

Step 3: Write 5 Supportive Goals

Choose goals that feel realistic. These are options, not promises.

Step 4: Personal Choice

Reflection:
Small choices, made consistently, change the direction of a life.

If you completed just one goal each day that moves you in the right direction, in one month you would have taken thirty meaningful steps forward.

Those steps add up. They shape your walk with God, strengthen your relationships, and slowly change the kind of person you are becoming.

You do not need to change everything today. You only need to choose one step and take it faithfully.

What is the ONE goal you are willing to complete today?

Stay focused. Stay present. Do the next right thing.

Direction matters more than speed. One step is enough.
