Intergenerational Trauma & Transmission

What We Inherit. What We Repeat. What We Redeem.

Class: ECTLC Life Lessons by Glenn Morgan

Intergenerational Transmission

Trauma is not just an event.

It becomes:

  • Emotional patterns
  • Nervous system wiring
  • Identity formation
  • Belief systems
  • Relational habits

What is not healed is handed down.

Generation 1 – My Father

False Narratives: Wheatboy story, Black Allergy story, My Uncle Michael's fight with the devil story.

Stories of a Pimp's life.

Graphic war stories told to a six-year-old.

Rum. Rage. Violence. Chaos.

Masculinity defined by domination, intimidation and destruction.

I absorbed identity before I had language.

The Confession

My Father was a racist, but confusingly most of his wives were White.

He never reached his considerable potential, so he lived through other people's lives.

He felt less than those around him so he used narratives to manipulate people around him.

He was a gifted story teller, but because of his shame and guilt, he turned to alcohol to mask his feelings.

He never went to Vietnam.

He went AWOL after boot camp.

The battlefield lived in his shame, not history.

But I still inherited the emotional war.

Generation 1 – My Grandfather

Education was non-negotiable.

Owned MD&N Electronics.

Developed radar systems detecting low-flying aircraft.

Honored aboard USS Little Rock during reopening of the Suez Canal.

He built infrastructure that protected nations.

BART Tunnels & Towers

At seven years old, I worked beside him.

Inside tunnels under the bay.

On towers on the top of hills.

Systems thinking was planted early.

Infrastructure lasts. Chaos collapses. Hope flurishes

Two Inheritances

Competence + Shame

Discipline + Rage

Long-term vision + Boom/Bust cycles

I carried both.

Generation 2 – My Life

Education. Leadership. Business success.

Leading large organizations.

But underneath...anger and unresolved shame.

A fragile foundation.

When my young family died suddenly, my Jen, my Conrad, then my best friend and then my Dad…

I always viewed my Father's life like a Tiger was stalking him, and I saw when it caught him and tore his life ascunder.

I carried that burden and life view with me...always. Waiting, fearing, agonizing the day when the other shoe would drop. That sense every second, every minute, every hour, until it finally happened.

How I Ended Up Here

I burned my whole life to the ground and lost my new family, and because of my own actions, pushed away my existing family, and lost them too.

Ability was never the issue.

Unhealed pain met catastrophic grief.

Intergenerational trauma finds cracks under pressure.

Generation 3 – My Daughters

I tried to break the transmission.

  • Jesus
  • Presence
  • Patience
  • Calm spirit
  • Guidance
  • Education
  • Stability
  • No rage

But habits and inner pain still leaked through.

We reduce transmission. We rarely eliminate it completely.

Biblical Anchor

Exodus 20:5–6 – Patterns travel generationally.

John 8:32 – Truth breaks false identity.

2 Corinthians 5:17 – New creation interrupts inheritance.

Trauma transmission is generational, but so is Redemption.

Your Turn

What are you repeating?
