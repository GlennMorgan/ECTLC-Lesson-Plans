Stress Management – Quick Class Guide

ECTLC Work Therapy Session | Facilitator: Irene McKinley

Start the Conversation

Ask: What is stress?

Write answers on board.

Three Types:

  • Physical – body reactions
  • Emotional – feelings
  • Cognitive – thoughts and beliefs

The Stress Cycle

Write:

Trigger → Thought → Feeling → Reaction → Result

Example:

  • Trigger: Someone puts you on the spot
  • Thought: “I can’t handle this”
  • Feeling: Anxiety
  • Reaction: Shut down / avoid / argue
  • Result: More stress

4 Reactions:

  • Fight – anger, control
  • Flight – avoidance, escape
  • Freeze – stuck, blank mind
  • Fawn – people-pleasing

Understanding Stress

Ask group: What do you feel when you are stressed?

Sort answers into:

  • Physical
  • Emotional
  • Cognitive

Key Insight

Disappointment = expectation minus reality

Ask:

Which one can you control?

Describe a fence:

  • Inside yard = what I control
  • Outside yard = what I don’t

The 4 P’s

  • Pause
  • Be Present
  • Be Patient
  • Pray

Healthy Coping

  • Deep breathing
  • Body scan
  • Short walk
  • Prayer
  • Talk to someone safe
  • Write what you can control

Unhealthy Coping

  • Avoidance
  • Isolation
  • Anger reactions
  • Overeating
  • Zoning out
  • Impulsive decisions
“Faith without works is dead.” — James 2:14-18 (ESV)

Stress tools only help when we practice them. Knowing them is the start. Using them changes outcomes.
